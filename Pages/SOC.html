Six of crows review


A book by Leigh Bardugo

★★★★☆ Six of crows is as it describes itself, an oceans 11 heist in a fantasy world, a captivating group of characters must come together to conduct a prison break. Each with their own flaws they struggle with, they must move past it and work together to complete their deal. An amazing fantasy book, I loved it. I highly recommend this book to anyone who likes the fantasy genre as a whole. It does something unique with the genre yet is a captivating read nonetheless.

I was scared to start Six of Crows. The hype surrounding it has been HUGE, the blurb sounded intriguing, I've been eagerly anticipating it, and I liked but didn't love Shadow and Bone. Honestly, though? I needn't have worried. This book is fantastic.


Elle ★★★★★

Five solid stars. This book pulled me in from the beginning, to the point where I knew I'd found a favorite before I was halfway through. Here, Bardugo integrates a creative plot, interesting and morally grey characters, group dynamics and banter, a great writing style, and some really, really prime romance plots. Goddamn, can Leigh Bardugo write romance. There are very, very few books that I love so wholeheartedly. This series truly is my favorite fantasy series of all time. There's no competition whatsoever.
