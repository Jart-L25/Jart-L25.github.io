Uzumaki Review


A horror manga by Junji Ito

★★★★☆ Uzumaki is a manga created by renowned horror manga artist, Junji Ito. This is a manga that explores a multitude of different horrors all revolving around the main characters home and a severe case of supernatural spirals. This manga I recommend as a must read for anyone who's into the horror genre as a whole. Despite the medium that may be unfamiliar to people from a western background like us, it's compelling story and amazing art puts it leagues above other horror media I've consumed.
